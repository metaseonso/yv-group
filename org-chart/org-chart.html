No reception · no photo line

The Custodian is the brand's reception

At LTP, Reception and Customer Support & Concierge stand between the people and the Custodian. At YV the relation to the brand is the work, so the founder and the brand's members go straight to their Custodian. What the desk did for money moves to YV itself.

Custodian · moved from the desk

What the Custodian does

  • Is the founder's one contact at YV, in a group of the founder, the official YV account and the Custodian (P3).
  • Runs the brand's seasons (P1) and keeps its members (P2).
  • Takes concerns from the founder and from members, decides and closes them (P5).
  • Books venues, production, food and printing for gatherings (P7).
YV · the company

What YV keeps

  • Weekly statements and Friday withdrawals, processed by the GMP or whom the GMP names (P8).
  • Invoices for gatherings, issued and recorded by YV (P7).
  • The member ledger: who joined, in which season, with which badges (P2).
  • A GMP check on spend and withdrawals of a kind listed in V-13.

Compensation · same split as LTP

The 25% Partner Share

The founder owns the brand. The residency belongs to YV and sits in the Custodian's portfolio. The Partner Share is 25% of the fees YV actually receives for a brand: the residency fee, season fees and any success fee. It is never paid on the brand's own sales. Half goes to the person who brought the brand in, and half to the person who looks after it.

Example · one brand, one week

Example figures. The amounts are credited to each person's Portfolio Account at the weekly consolidation and can be withdrawn on Fridays. Equity a brand grants YV waits on V-16.

Pay is credited weekly

P1 · New

The Season

We don't just go to market. Each season the Custodian takes the brand through seven steps, and each season starts larger than the last: more people, more frequent story beats, and more that early members have earned.

P1.1Warm circle

The first early-access people come from warm connections, the brand's and ours.

P1.2Focus group

They use the product and are heard. Results become case studies, published only with the brand's consent.

P1.3Gathering

Communal gatherings. The strategic narrative and its branching storylines are taken from what happens there.

P1.4Story beat

One movement story beat, built from those storylines, that gets people moving to join.

P1.5Early access

The program opens; the first loyal customers join. Focus-group members get early-bird benefits.

P1.6Badges and perks

Commemorative badges and perks reward active participation, and compound with each new capability.

P1.7Season close

The Custodian and the founder read what grew and set the next season's scale.

Where LTP's protocols break, and what YV does

Eight points, cleared by Seonso on 21 November 2026. Each is written into this chart.

C1 · Cleared

The early-access loop has no LTP counterpart

P1 in seven steps, one season is one pass; P2 keeps the members between seasons.

C2 · Cleared

Desk duties have no desk

To the Custodian: founder requests (P3), concerns (P5), gathering vendors (P7). Statements and withdrawals go to YV (P8).

C3 · Cleared

Desk and photo protocols

Removed: B-03, B-04, B-18, the office roles and the Reception Booking Commission.

C4 · Cleared, then changed

The Support Share was the photographer's

Removed. No Support role and no 5% Support Share: the Custodian keeps the whole 12.5%.

C5 · Cleared

Fee Income said "never rent"

Residency, season and success fees received; never the brand's sales. Equity a brand grants YV: V-16.

C6 · Cleared

Who gives and keeps the perks

Perks are the brand's, written into the season plan. Badges are never taken back. YV keeps the member ledger.

C7 · Cleared

A Friday meeting of contractors

Open, not compulsory. Required attendance can make a contractor look like an employee.

C8 · Cleared

Ladder and shares

A Partner holds a YV share, as at LTP. Tiers numbered 1 to 4, with no employee ladder below.

Part C · everyone

Rules for everyone

While at YV and for 3 years after

Confidentiality

Keep brand plans, founder conversations, member lists, pricing, systems, financials and partnership terms confidential.

While at YV and for 2 years after

No competing, no poaching

Do not work for a direct competitor in the markets V-17 names, take YV brands or members away, or recruit YV people.

From the day they are made

Work product

Story materials, case studies and the member ledger made for YV belong to YV. The brand's name, marks and product stay the brand's.
