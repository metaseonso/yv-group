Organisation chart

We take brands into residence and go to market with them. Every brand is held in one Partner's portfolio, and that Partner is the brand's own person at YV, season after season.

Part A · One ladder

From Contractor to General Managing Partner

Everyone starts as a contractor and earns the Partner Share on their brands from the day they sign. Above Partner, the ladder is the same as LTP's.

  1. Tier 4

    General Managing Partner

    Appointed from the Managing Partners. Runs the office, sets the variables, approves spend under V-13.

    PayOffice share (V-06) of the office's Fee Income; other pay V-05.

  2. Tier 3

    Managing Partner

    Partner KPIs met for consecutive quarters (V-04), a market plan defended, elevated by the GMP. Manages new contractors and contracts; assigns brands.

    PayOwn Partner Share on own brands; override V-15, if any.

  3. Tier 2

    Partner

    Six months after signing, one YV share bought, advancement review passed.

    PayThe 25% Partner Share on each brand they source or hold.

  4. Tier 1

    Contractor

    40 hours of training (20 at gatherings, 20 on a live season beside a Partner), then the Partner Agreement is signed.

    PayThe 25% Partner Share from day one. No salary.

Not at YV: Employee Track, Real Estate Photographer Track, Reception, Customer Support & Concierge. Partners are the brand's reception: the founder speaks to their Custodian, not to a desk.

A3 · Brands in residence

Three roles on a brand

The founder owns the brand. The residency is YV's mandate, under the Residency Agreement, to take that brand to market with its people, seasons and gatherings. Each residency sits in exactly one Custodian's portfolio.

Sourcing Share12.5%

Sourcer

Brought the brand to YV: introduced the founder and got the Residency Agreement signed.

Custodian Share · Support inside12.5%

Custodian

Holds the brand in their portfolio and is its direct relation at YV. Runs its seasons, cares for the founder, answers its members, handles disputes.

Support Share · from the Custodian5%

Support

Anyone assigned to help on the brand's seasons, such as a gathering host, a creator or a producer. Default 5%; the Custodian may agree 0% to 12.5% in writing.

P1 · New · proposed

The Season

We don't just go to market. Each season the Custodian runs one pass of these seven steps with the brand, and each pass starts larger than the last: more people, more frequent story beats, more that early members have earned.

  1. Warm circle.

    The first early-access people come from warm connections, the brand's and ours.

  2. Focus group.

    They use the product and are heard. What they find becomes case studies, published only with the brand's consent.

  3. Gathering.

    Communal gatherings with the focus group. The brand's strategic narrative and its branching storylines are taken from what happens there.

  4. Story beat.

    One movement story beat, built from those storylines, that gets people moving to join early access.

  5. Early access.

    The program opens and the first loyal customers join. Focus-group members get early-bird benefits.

  6. Badges and perks.

    Commemorative badges and perks reward active participation, and compound as each new capability is added.

  7. Season close.

    The Custodian and the founder read what grew and set the next season's scale.

Part F · For Seonso

Where LTP's protocols break, and what we propose

Eight points to clear. Each is written into the chart above as proposed; a cleared point becomes the chart.

C1

The early-access loop has no LTP counterpart

Proposed: P1 in seven steps, one season is one pass; P2 keeps the members between seasons.

C2

Desk duties have no desk

Proposed: founder requests (P3), concerns (P5) and gathering vendors (P7) go to the Custodian. Statements and withdrawals go to YV (P8).

C3

Desk and photo protocols

Proposed: remove B-03, B-04, B-18, the office (A2.5) and the Reception Booking Commission (A2.6).

C4

The Support Share was the photographer's

Proposed: keep it at 5% default inside the Custodian Share, for whoever is assigned as Support.

C5

Fee Income said "never rent"

Proposed: residency, season and success fees received; never the brand's sales. Equity a brand grants YV: set later as V-16.

C6

Who gives and keeps the perks

Proposed: perks are the brand's, written into the season plan. Badges are never taken back. YV keeps the member ledger.

C7

A Friday meeting of contractors

Proposed: open to all, not compulsory.

C8

Ladder and shares

Proposed: a Partner holds a YV share, as at LTP. Tiers numbered 1 to 4, since there is no employee ladder below.

Part C

Rules for everyone

During and 3 years after

Confidentiality

Brand plans, founder conversations, member lists, pricing, systems, financials and partnership terms.

During and 2 years after

No competing

No work for a business competing directly with YV's brand residencies, in the markets V-17 names.

During and 2 years after

No poaching

No taking YV brands or members to a competitor, and no recruiting YV people.

From the day made

Work product

Story materials, case studies and the member ledger belong to YV. The brand's name, marks and product stay the brand's.
