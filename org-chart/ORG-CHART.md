### A3. Brands in residence

**Brand and residency.** The founder owns the brand. The residency is YV's: YV's mandate, under the Residency Agreement with the founder, to take the brand to market with its people, seasons and gatherings. Each residency is held in the portfolio of exactly one Custodian, or by YV while it has none. The Partner Share, Fee Income and seasons are counted per brand.

Roles on a brand, recorded at intake (P4):

- **Sourcer:** brought the brand to YV: introduced the founder and got the signed Residency Agreement.
- **Custodian:** holds the brand in their portfolio and is the brand's direct relation at YV: runs its seasons, cares for the founder, answers its members, handles disputes. A Contractor can be a Custodian.

One person may hold more than one role. A Managing Partner or the GMP assigns the Custodian. There is no Support role.

### A4. The 25% Partner Share

**Fee Income:** the residency fee, season fees and any success fee YV actually receives for a brand. **Never the brand's own sales.** Equity or tokens a brand grants YV: {V-16}.

| Share | % of Fee Income | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Sourcing Share | 12.5% | Sourcer |
| Custodian Share | 12.5% | Custodian |
| **Total** | **25%** | |

There is no Support Share: the Custodian keeps the whole 12.5% (Seonso, 2026-11-21: "support can be removed from the chart. 5%"). Leaving and changing, and the weekly clock, are LTP's A4.4 and A4.5 with "listing" read as "brand": shares follow the person or return to YV as LTP's table says; Fee Income is consolidated weekly at {V-12}, credited to each Portfolio Account, withdrawn on Fridays. Partners receive no base pay unless the GMP sets one {V-07}.

**P1 The Season** — *Custodian, with the brand.* One season is one pass through seven steps. Each season repeats at a larger scale and with more frequent story beats; the length and number of seasons a year is {V-08}.

1. **Warm circle.** Draw the first early-access people from warm connections, the brand's and YV's.
2. **Focus group.** They use the product and are heard. Results are recorded as case studies, published only with the brand's consent.
3. **Gathering.** Communal gatherings with the focus group. The brand's strategic narrative and its branching storylines are taken from what happens there.
4. **Story beat.** One movement story beat, built from those storylines, that gets people moving to join early access.
5. **Early access.** The program opens. The first loyal customers join; focus-group members get early-bird benefits.
6. **Badges and perks.** Commemorative badges and perks reward active participation. Benefits compound as each new capability is added.
7. **Season close.** The Custodian and the founder read what grew and set the next season's scale.

**P2 Members and badges** — *Custodian; YV keeps the ledger.* Everyone who joins through a season is recorded as a member with the season they joined. A badge, once earned, is never taken back. Perks are the brand's to give, written into the season plan, and an early bird's benefits carry into every later season.

**P3 Duty of care** — *Custodian.* The Custodian is the brand's direct relation; nothing about the brand goes through a desk. The Custodian opens a group with exactly the founder, the official YV account and the Custodian within {V-09} days of assignment, answers the founder within {V-10} hours, and checks in every week of a season.

**P4 Intake and Brand Packet** — *Sourcer.* Before a residency starts: the signed Residency Agreement; the brand story as the founder tells it; the product's state; what the first season should reach; the brand's name, marks and assets; known limits. Sourcer and Custodian are recorded. An incomplete packet does not start.

**P5 Brand and member concerns** — *Custodian.* Concerns from the founder or from members come to the Custodian directly. The Custodian answers, decides, carries it out or assigns it, and records it closed. A brand with no Custodian is handled by YV.

**P6 Disputes and stakeholder relations** — *Custodian.* As LTP B-06; unresolved after {V-11} days goes to a Managing Partner or the GMP.

**P7 Gatherings: vendors and invoicing** — *Custodian; YV invoices.* The Custodian books the venues, production, food and printing a season plan calls for. YV issues and records the invoices. Spend that {V-13} marks waits for GMP approval.

**P8 Weekly consolidation, statements, withdrawals** — *YV.* As LTP B-15. With no concierge, the GMP or whom the GMP names processes Friday withdrawals; any that {V-13} marks wait for GMP approval.

**P9 Training before signing** — *Managing Partner.* 40 hours before signing the Partner Agreement: 20 at gatherings, 20 on a live season beside a Partner. The record is filed with the agreement.

**P10 Friday meeting** — *Everyone.* A team meeting every Friday, open to all and not compulsory: everyone at YV is a contractor, and compulsory attendance can make a contractor look like an employee.

**P11–P18** — As LTP B-09, B-10, B-11, B-12, B-13, B-14, B-16, B-17, with YV for LTP, Tier 3 for Tier 5, and KPI targets {V-01} read from season outcomes.

## Part C — Restrictions

1. **Confidentiality:** brand plans, founder conversations, member lists, pricing, systems, financials and partnership terms, during engagement and for 3 years after.
2. **No competing:** during engagement and for 2 years after, no work for a business that competes directly with YV's brand residency services in {V-17}.
3. **No poaching:** during engagement and for 2 years after, no taking YV brands or members to a competitor, no recruiting YV people.
4. **Work product:** story materials, case studies and the member ledger made for YV belong to YV. The brand's name, marks and product stay the brand's.

## Part E — Terms

- **Brand:** the founder's business and its product. The founder owns it.
- **Residency:** YV's mandate under the Residency Agreement to take a brand to market. Held in one Custodian's portfolio, or by YV.
- **Portfolio:** the brands a person holds as Custodian.
- **Season:** one pass of P1.
- **Member:** a person who joined through a season; recorded under P2.
- **Fee Income, Partner Share, Portfolio Account, weekly consolidation, Formal Notice:** as LTP, with brand for listing.
